# Build Dom's Big List Inside Coinbase

*Educational research as of September 30, 2026, not personalized financial advice. Almost every price, market cap and listing status below comes from search-result snippets gathered while Coinbase.com, Robinhood.com, Kraken, DefiLlama, CoinGecko, Yahoo Finance and stockanalysis were blocked for direct reading. Before every buy, check the ticker, the price and whether it can actually be traded, live in the app.*

You can run almost all of Dom's $1,000 a month from one Coinbase account. Coinbase has offered **commission-free US stock and ETF trading, with fractional shares, 24 hours a day five days a week, to all US users since February 2026** ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/391016/coinbase-yahoo-finance-stock-trading-all-users)). Its crypto list covers nearly every non-obvious token worth owning. The master list below ranks **about 50 names**: roughly 30 stocks and 20 tokens, grouped into three risk tiers. The strongest ideas are the ones where cash flow is real and the crowd has not piled in yet. On the stock side, that means cheaper AI "plumbing" (**Semtech, FormFactor, Penguin, Modine, Powell**), government-backed industrials (**Centrus, Planet Labs, Redwire**) and bitcoin miners turned AI landlords (**IREN, TeraWulf**). On the crypto side, it means the few tokens that actually pass revenue to holders (**HYPE, LINK, AAVE**), with SOL and TAO as beaten-down, higher-beta holdings. Fees are the biggest trap in the app. A Coinbase recurring buy costs about **2.49% all-in**, while a limit order on Coinbase Advanced costs **0.40-0.60% with no spread** ([Datawallet](https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/coinbase-fees)). Buying crypto the lazy way would cost roughly four times as much every month, for decades. Several of the hottest September names have already made their move: **ZEC is up about 2,500%, SUI rose about 70% in two weeks, and ENA faces a 14%-of-supply unlock on October 5**. They go on the "wait for a pullback" list, not the buy list. One thing Coinbase does not appear to offer is a Roth IRA. If Dom has earned income, the single best upgrade is to route about $625 a month through a Roth at Robinhood or another broker and keep the rest in Coinbase.

Every stock below trades on Nasdaq or NYSE and should therefore be on Robinhood. **Whether each small cap is on Coinbase's US list was not verified.** If a search in the Coinbase app comes up empty, buy that name at Robinhood.

## The $1,000 monthly plan runs on Coinbase Advanced

**The fee fix comes first.** Coinbase recurring buys use Simple-trade pricing: about **1.99% on buys over $200 plus a spread of about 0.5%, roughly 2.49% in total**, and the spread can widen to about 2% when markets are volatile ([CryptoRyancy](https://www.cryptoryancy.com/coinbase-recurring-buy-fees/)). **Coinbase Advanced charges 0.60% taker or 0.40% maker below $10K of monthly volume, with no spread** ([Datawallet](https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/coinbase-fees)). On $200 a month of crypto, that is about **$5 a month on recurring buys versus $0.80-1.20 on Advanced**. Over 20 years the difference compounds into thousands of dollars.

**Coinbase One Basic ($4.99 a month) is a bad deal at this size.** It waives the fee but not the spread ([CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/what-is-coinbase-one-zero-fee-subscription/)), so on $200 of crypto the subscription alone costs 2.5%.

**The monthly routine has five steps:**

1. Deposit USD on payday.
2. Open Advanced.
3. For each token, place a limit order a few cents below the current price, marked "post-only", so it fills at the 0.40% maker fee.

## Written sell rules beat picking the right ticker

These rules exist because the base rates are brutal. The top **4.3% of US stocks produced all net stock-market wealth creation since 1926** ([SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2900447)). **Over 40% of Russell 3000 stocks suffered permanent drops of 70% or more** ([J.P. Morgan](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/content/dam/jpm-wm-aem/global/pb/en/insights/eye-on-the-market/eotm-the-agony-and-the-ecstasy.pdf)).

**Position caps, measured at cost:**
- No stock above **6%** and no tier-3 stock above **2%**.
- **HYPE at most 7%**, LINK, AAVE and SOL at most 4% each, and TAO and every other token at most 2%. Tier-3 AI or DePIN tokens stay at 1% or less.
- **Everything crypto-linked, miners included, at most 30%.**
- When a name reaches its cap, send its monthly dollars to the most underweight sleeve.

**Trimming winners:**
- Trim only when a position grows to **2-3x its target weight**, and sell just the excess back into VTI. Do not sell a stock simply because it doubled.
- Tier-3 names are the exception. On a 3x, sell a third to recover the cost basis and let the rest ride.
- In any crypto name that goes parabolic the way ZEC did, trim a quarter each time the price doubles.
- In the taxable account, try to hold 12 months first, so gains get long-term rates of 0% or 15% ([Tax Foundation](https://taxfoundation.org/data/all/federal/2026-tax-brackets/)).
