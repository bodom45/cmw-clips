## Which of these have accelerating fundamentals vs pure narrative in 2026?

### Takeaway
Accelerating fundamentals, in order of evidence strength: Bloom (+166%), Nebius (+454%), CoreWeave (+112%), Modine data center (+90%), Rocket Lab (+62%), SoFi (+40%), FormFactor (+32% with rising margins), nLight (+34%), and Robinhood (+32%). The miners (IREN, WULF) are fundamentally real but still ramping. Contracts are signed; revenue lags. The pure-narrative names are Rigetti, D-Wave, Richtech, NuScale, and to a large degree AST SpaceMobile and IonQ, whose growth is flattered by an acquisition. Coinbase and Fluence are going the wrong way near term.
