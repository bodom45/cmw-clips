## Which tokens on Coinbase US have real revenue or usage growth in 2026?

### Takeaway
Real, growing cash flow that reaches token holders is rare. Several tokens clear the bar:
- **HYPE**: #1 in 2026 revenue, with about 99% of it going to buybacks. It has been listed on Coinbase spot since Feb 5, 2026.
- **LINK**: the reserve has passed 6M LINK, bought with revenue.
- **AAVE**: $333M of 2026 year-to-date revenue plus buybacks, though revenue is down from 2025.
- **ZEC**: usage-driven rather than revenue-driven.
- **ONDO**: TVL growth and a BlackRock tie-up, but weak token value capture.

A second group has real products but thin or no token value capture: ENA (the fee switch is approved but inactive until USDe reaches $7.5B), MORPHO (big fees, none go to token holders), SUI, SOL, AERO, and HNT. Most AI tokens (FET, VIRTUAL, RENDER, AKT, and TAO to a degree) are narrative-driven, with small verified revenue.
