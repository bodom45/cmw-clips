## Which themes have strong policy/secular tailwinds in 2026?

### Takeaway
US government money is flowing clearly to defense drones and unmanned systems, domestic nuclear fuel (HALEU), and critical minerals and rare earths (loans from DoD's Office of Strategic Capital and EXIM). Grid storage demand is real (hyperscaler deals), but Eos's stock collapse shows the tailwind alone does not guarantee returns.

### Cited Findings
- The Pentagon's FY2027 budget request includes nearly $75B for unmanned systems and counter-drone technology — [Yahoo Finance / Investing.com analysis](https://www.investing.com/analysis/drone-stocks-are-down-but-defense-backlogs-tell-a-different-story-200683545)
- The Trump administration launched a $1B "Drone Dominance" program in late 2025 — [US News](https://money.usnews.com/investing/articles/best-drone-stocks-to-buy)
- New US drone tariffs (Aug 2026) shield US drone makers from foreign rivals; Kratos and AeroVironment rose, and small caps Red Cat and Ondas were also affected — [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/08/61205678/trumps-new-drone-tariffs-shield-us-makers-from-foreign-rivals-sending-defense-giants-kratos-and-aerovironment-higher-with-small-caps-red-cat-ondas)
- On June 18, 2026, Energy Fuels received a conditional $725M financing commitment from the DoD Office of Strategic Capital for critical-minerals processing at White Mesa and a rare earth metals/alloys facility — [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/trading-ideas/movers/26/06/53281203/pentagon-bets-725m-on-energy-fuels-5-rare-earth-stocks-to-watch)
- Perpetua secured a $2.9B, 13-year US EXIM loan (May 2026) for Stibnite, the only planned domestic antimony source; the Department of War identified it as the only US mine able to meet defense antimony demand by 2029 — [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/trading-ideas/movers/26/06/53281203/pentagon-bets-725m-on-energy-fuels-5-rare-earth-stocks-to-watch); [Mugglehead](https://mugglehead.com/perpetua-resources-advances-stibnite-construction-amid-us-antimony-push/)
- DOE selected Centrus for a $900M HALEU enrichment award, and Centrus officially launched its centrifuge build-out in Q4 2025 — [Centrus 8-K FY2025 results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065059/000162828026006909/ex991-10k2026_02x10.htm)
- NRC approved Oklo's Principal Design Criteria for Aurora (Sept 2026); Oklo is worth ~$7.1B and NuScale ~$4.0B — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/top-3-nuclear-stocks-watch-061313408.html)
- Fluence reported a record backlog and hyperscaler deals, but missed estimates in its fiscal Q2 2026 — [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/fluence-q2-2026-slides-record-backlog-hyperscaler-deals-offset-miss-93CH-4669109)
- Humanoid robot market estimated at $5.41B in 2026 (industry forecast, treat as promotional) — [MarketsandMarkets](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/humanoid-robot-market-99567653.html)

### Inferences
- Where there is direct government money (drones, HALEU, antimony and rare earths), the thesis rests on contracts and loans. Where the demand story is purely "AI data-center power" (SMRs), it rests mostly on sentiment. Nuclear equities appear to have de-rated sharply from 2025 highs (Centrus -54% in one year).
- Robotics small caps are mostly either expensive (VPG +269% YTD) or supplier "picks and shovels" plays. Robotics looks weaker for asymmetric small-cap picks than defense or critical minerals.

### Inferences (failure modes by name)
- **LEU:** Delays or cost overruns on the Piketon centrifuge build; more equity raises; dependence on DOE funding; policy on Russian LEU supply; the contingent backlog may not convert.

- **RCAT:** Failing to hit the H2 ramp; thin margins; SRR program competition; dilution.
- **PPTA:** Construction overruns; gold and antimony price; permitting litigation.
- **NNE/Oklo/SMR:** No revenue for years; NRC timelines; heavy dilution. These are hype-sensitive.
- **CAPR:** A CRL or narrow label would cause a large drop; a single-asset company.
