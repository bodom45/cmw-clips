What is this?

A live music competition inside a 16,000 sq ft art gallery. 20 Dallas artists, one song each, all backed by the same live house band — Tiny Desk style, no backing tracks.

You walk the gallery, catch sets on five stages, and at the end of the night you help decide who moves on.
