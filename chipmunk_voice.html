🐿️ Chipmunk Voice

Sing into your mic (straight in or through your Rane) and hear yourself as a chipmunk through your speakers, live.

Click start, then allow microphone access.

Audio gear

Device names show up after you click Start and allow the mic. To hear the chipmunk through the speakers on your Rane, set the output to the Rane and switch a mixer channel's input to USB / PC. If you hear your normal voice too, that's the Rane sending the mic straight to the speakers, so turn the mic down in the mixer's main mix.

Setup

🎧 Headphones give the cleanest, lowest-lag sound. On speakers, keep the mic away from them or turn on Speaker mode.
