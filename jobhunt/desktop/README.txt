JOB HUNT

1. Double-click "Start Applying" (Mac: the first time, right-click it and choose Open).
2. It downloads this morning's job list and opens the best matches one by one, pre-filled.
3. Check the form, answer any extra questions, solve the captcha, press Submit.
4. Back in the black window, type  a  (applied),  s  (skip) or  q  (quit), then Enter.

To change your resume, drop a new PDF into this folder named resume.pdf.
To change your details, open profile.json in TextEdit.
